How to Convert CGPA to Percentage (and Back)

If you studied at an Indian university or school, you've likely come across the term CGPA.

The Standard CGPA-to-Percentage Formula

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

For example, if your CGPA is 8.4, your percentage is 79.8%.

Converting Percentage Back to CGPA

CGPA = Percentage ÷ 9.5

For example, if you scored 85%, your CGPA is roughly 8.95.

Summary Table

  • CGPA 10.0 → 95%
  • CGPA 9.0 → 85.5%
  • CGPA 8.0 → 76%
  • CGPA 7.0 → 66.5%
  • CGPA 6.0 → 57%
