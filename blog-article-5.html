7 Mental Math Tricks for Calculating Percentages Fast

You don't always have a calculator in your hand — but percentages show up everywhere.

Trick 1: The 10% Rule

To find 10% of any number, just move the decimal point one place to the left.

  • 10% of 250 = 25
  • 10% of 47 = 4.7

Trick 2: 5% Is Half of 10%

  • 5% of 80 → 10% is 8 → half is 4

Trick 3: Swap the Numbers

X% of Y is the same as Y% of X.

  • 16% of 25 = ? → Flip it → 25% of 16 = 4

Trick 4: Use Easy Fractions

  • 25% = 1/4 → 25% of 40 = 40 ÷ 4 = 10
  • 50% = 1/2 → 50% of 90 = 90 ÷ 2 = 45
