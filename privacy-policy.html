Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quick Percentage Calculator handles your information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

Our calculator tools operate entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not collect, store, or transmit any numbers or personal data you enter into the calculators.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

This website may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. The DoubleClick DART cookie is used by Google to serve ads to visitors.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or www.aboutads.info.

3. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

If you are a resident of the EEA or California, you have rights regarding your personal data. Because we do not directly collect personal data, these rights are handled by our advertising partners.

4. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, email us at support@quickpercentagecalculator.com.
