Percentage Increase vs. Percentage Points: What's the Difference?

News headlines often confuse two very different concepts: percentage increase and percentage points.

The Core Difference

  • Percentage point: The arithmetic difference between two percentages. (e.g., 3% to 5% is a 2 percentage point increase).
  • Percentage increase: The relative growth. (e.g., 3% to 5% is a 66.7% increase).

Which Should You Use?

Use percentage points when comparing two percentages directly (like polling data). Use percentage increase/decrease when talking about growth relative to the starting value.
