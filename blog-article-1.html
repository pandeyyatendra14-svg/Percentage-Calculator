How to Calculate Percentage: A Complete Beginner's Guide

Percentages are one of the most useful math concepts you will ever learn. They appear everywhere — in exam scores, shopping sales, bank interest rates, and news headlines.

What Is a Percentage?

The word "percentage" comes from the Latin per centum, meaning "by the hundred." A percentage is simply a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100.

The Universal Percentage Formula

Percentage (%) = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

Where "Part" is the specific amount you're measuring and "Whole" is the total.

Example 1: Converting Marks to Percentage

  • Part = 72 (marks obtained)
  • Whole = 90 (total marks)
  • Percentage = (72 ÷ 90) × 100 = 0.8 × 100 = 80%

Example 2: Finding a Percentage of a Number

  • Percentage = 15
  • Whole = 240
  • Part = (15 ÷ 100) × 240 = 0.15 × 240 = 36
